English (working translation):
I recall that, among the other honours that [Your Lordship] did me at Nikolsburg, I am confident I gave this
Most Serene [Queen] to understand that [you] would confer a canonry of Olmütz on one of [her] sons. Having
[since] learned at [this] court that that intention of [Your Lordship's] has not [yet] been carried out, as
these Majesties desire, I have judged it the duty of a devoted servant, such as I am to her, to beg you, as I
do, to see [this ...] carried into effect, [or] first to deign to give me at once a kind answer about it.
