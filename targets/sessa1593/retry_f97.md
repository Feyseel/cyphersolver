# Retry with the full nomenclature of fr. 3995 f. 97 (2 Oct 2026)

`key92.tsv` held 40 word entries of the sheet, up to *satisfecho*; the nomenclature on f. 97 (canvas 188) has over a hundred
(count first made by satorunet, 3 Oct 2026). The sheet was read again from the Gallica image and 61 entries added to `key92.tsv` (
below the comment block); eight more, whose bare number is also a syllable (24 podra, 31 servicio, 34 siempre, 49 Su Santidad, 55 tambien, 63 tierra, 57e tanta, 67e tratar), are kept there as comments, because the table looks tokens up by the base number and the dots or the T of the sheet are not in it. The endorsement on the back, f. 97v (canvas 189), reads "1592 Chifre d'entre le Duc de Parme et le Roy
Cat.co en l'an 1592, lequel sert aussi pour le Duc de Sessa et Don Diego de Ybarre avec le Roy": it labels this very key, and it
confirms that nos. 45, 47 and 79 (Miranda, Sessa) are in it. f. 98 (canvas 190) is another key ("Chiffre de monsieur du Maine ...
1592"): the 19 Sept NOTES put the "Chifre d'entre le Duc de Parme" endorsement at f. 98, which was wrong.

Rule sheet at the right of f. 97 ("Autres"): a mark over a group: ^ = a following s; ᵖ-hook = an r between the two characters;
a bar-e below = an a at the end; o = an s between two characters; ṅ = an l; two dots over a group of doubled letters = the e at the end;
a dot over a group = l ("fait une l"). These agree with the diacritic values the readings already used.

## A check of the f. 97 key on satorunet's fr. 3641 no. 58 (f. 124v), 3 Oct 2026

satorunet's page https://satoru.net/crypt/ibarra1592/ reads the 26 cipher lines of f. 124v (BnF fr. 3641 no. 58, Feria to Idiáquez,
7 April 1593) with this key. His transcription of the lines (`f124v.json` on his site) was decoded here with `decode92.py` and the extended
`key92.tsv`, without his readings: the key reproduces **1,005 of the 1,430 letters of his readings in runs of four or more (70%)**;
the same signs shuffled within each line give 226 on average (maximum 275 in 300 shuffles). For the 11 lines he marks high confidence:
494 of 688 (72%) against a shuffled maximum of 133. Two lines (v14 and v26) were compared with the Gallica image (btv1b52508089f f266)
sign by sign and agree with his transcription. That is a check of the transcription and of the key together, not of every word of his
reading: the groups marked open on his page (Xoz, Xut, Yus, guap, aum, du, ms, 86T, 51T) are not in the f. 97 sheet either.
His count of the nomenclature entries, and the values he listed from it, were checked on the sheet; they agree with what is read here.
