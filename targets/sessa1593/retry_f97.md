# Retry with the full nomenclature of fr. 3995 f. 97 (2 Oct 2026)

`key92.tsv` held 40 word entries of the sheet, up to *satisfecho*; the nomenclature on f. 97 (canvas 188) has over a hundred
(count first made by satorunet, 3 Oct 2026). The sheet was read again from the Gallica image and 70 entries added (`key92.tsv`,
below the comment block). The endorsement on the back, f. 97v (canvas 189), reads "1592 Chifre d'entre le Duc de Parme et le Roy
Cat.co en l'an 1592, lequel sert aussi pour le Duc de Sessa et Don Diego de Ybarre avec le Roy": it labels this very key, and it
confirms that nos. 45, 47 and 79 (Miranda, Sessa) are in it. f. 98 (canvas 190) is another key ("Chiffre de monsieur du Maine ...
1592"): the 19 Sept NOTES put the "Chifre d'entre le Duc de Parme" endorsement at f. 98, which was wrong.

Rule sheet at the right of f. 97 ("Autres"): a mark over a group: ^ = a following s; ᵖ-hook = an r between the two characters;
a bar-e below = an a at the end; o = an s between two characters; ṅ = an l; two dots over a group of doubled letters = the e at the end;
a dot over a group = l ("fait une l"). These agree with the diacritic values the readings already used.
