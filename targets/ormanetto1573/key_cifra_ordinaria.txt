Spain nunciature 'cifra ordinaria' (rules: Meister 1906 p. 265), table reconstructed by Ajaydas Devadas, 1 Oct 2026; checked in dec_aj.py.
Two signs per letter. Notation: d. = digit with a dot over it; 0.d = dotted zero followed by d; d0 = d closed by an undotted zero; 7: = two dots over 7.
Zero rule: an undotted 0 closes the digit before it, a dotted 0 opens the next digit; every other digit stands alone.
Word end: 2, 4 or 5 with a cross-stroke (DECODE writes the stroke as a digit 1 after those digits).
