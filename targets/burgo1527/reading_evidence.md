# R9970: limited reading evidence from the attached scans

These excerpts document a probable source mismatch. They are not a decipherment of Andrea del Burgo's letter. Square brackets mark expansion or uncertainty; `[…]` means omitted/unread text, with no implied length. Deleted wording is omitted unless stated. Orthography is lightly normalized; this is not a diplomatic edition.

## Archive page 1 (DECODE image P2)

Heading: **al principe**.

Secure phrases from the opening, separated where reading is incomplete:

> demás de los cambios q[ue] hasta aquí […]
> por n[uest]ro mandado […]
> […] avisado con los correos pasados […]

The revised body contains an amount written with Roman numerals and a thousands sign. An initial reading of the sign run as possible cipher was withdrawn after comparison with the second draft.

## Archive page 2, second draft (DECODE image P3)

Heading again: **al principe**.

> demás de los cambios q[ue] hasta aq[uí] se an hecho, a convenido
> hazer otro de CX [thousands sign] DCC LXX [currency abbreviation] de a LXV [gruesos?] […]

The thousands sign looks like a large U. Read as an amount, `CX × 1000 + DCCLXX = 110,770`. The currency abbreviation is not securely expanded, and *gruesos* is tentative. This arithmetic interpretation is supported by “cambios” and “hazer otro de,” and by the same expression in the first draft. It is not a cipher-key result.

## What these excerpts establish

The photographed item has two revised versions of a Spanish financial draft addressed to a prince. No signature, date, or salutation establishing the supplied Burgo/Gattinara identification was found on the four scanned surfaces. Difficult words and abbreviations remain; no full-text reading percentage is claimed.
