## Summary (modern English)

Archduke Albert, sovereign of the Spanish Netherlands, answers in writing what Licentiate **Morreus**, the agent of a
prince he calls "E. L." and "des Herzogen L." (the Duke of Bavaria, as head of the Catholic League), has put to him
orally and in writing. He thanks the prince for commending to him the preservation of the Emperor ("our most gracious,
beloved lord and cousin, his Imperial Highness") and of the interests of "our most praiseworthy House", and promises
the same correspondence in return.

On the substance: he agrees that the **recovery and reconquest of the kingdom [of Bohemia]** and the other lands calls
for every possible effort; the Catholic princes and Estates should give help and assistance to that end, and he has
already done good offices with the Emperor and the King [of Spain] and awaits further answer. Meanwhile he has resolved
that, should the worst happen, the troops he has now on foot will be sent up with orders to join the Catholic army and
obey its commander, and that Hungarian troops [being sent by the other side?] be countered by help for the oppressed
Catholic Estates. A **muster place, a regiment of foot and five hundred [horse]** he cannot grant at present, though
he would like to: his own levies are destined for **Luxembourg**, because of its situation. Since [Archduke Leopold?]
hesitates to garrison the fortress of **Breisach** with the [Elector's?] troops, Albert writes by this ordinary post to
the King and to **Oñate** (the Spanish ambassador in Vienna). For the **thousand cuirassiers** that the League asks to
raise in the name of the King [of Spain], he writes to **Ambrogio [Spinola]** and refers the Duke to him to confer.
If the Duke names qualified officers not in active command, Albert will give satisfaction. Dated 15 December 1619;
endorsed as Albert's *Resolutio* given to Licentiate Morreo.

The letter is the answer to the mission that R9426 (Ferdinand of Cologne, 22 December 1619) reports on: there the
agent "Morres" is named and the League's quarters and levies in the Rhine–Westphalian circle are the subject.
