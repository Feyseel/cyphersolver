Sense: Lanssac fears that the Emperor will forestall them and that the King of Denmark, bending to the Emperor's will,
could make trouble at the Sound, less than half a league wide and fortified on both sides; going by land would put
Monsieur [Anjou] at the mercy of the German princes, who are "extremely sorry" to see themselves shut in between two
such powerful kings; the road by Italy and Dalmatia is too long and costly; the Polish lords will ask him to bring 4,000
Gascons; Catherine should gather all the money she can so that when the embassy comes to bring the crown he is not kept
waiting, and should order the ships and galleys made ready. Of the lords there only four are content(?), but the whole
nobility cries out for a king.
