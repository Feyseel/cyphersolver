# ff. 182-189 — Lanssac's four letters of 9 May 1573 from Płock (BnF fr. 4735)

Gallica btv1b9060724s canvases 341-353, fetched 2 Oct 2026 at 1400 px (img/prev/q341-q353.jpg) and checked page by page.

**None of the four carries any cipher, and none has a marginal decipherment.** NOTES §8 listed them as letters "in the
same key"; that was an inference from the catalogue, not from the images. They are wholly in clear, so there is nothing
to decode and nothing for the read bar to measure (outcome for these documents: not applicable).

| folio | canvas | to | opening | cipher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 182r-v (address 183v "Au Roy de Pologne") | 341-343 | Henri duc d'Anjou, now King of Poland | "Sire, si je ne vous escrips l'heur que Dieu ha donné à toute la Republique chrestienne, au Roy, à vous, à la Royne …" | none |
| 184r (address 185v "A la Reyne Mere du Roy") | 344-346 | Catherine de Médicis | "Madame, …" (short letter announcing the election) | none |
| 186r (address 187v "A Monsieur Monsieur Brulart, conseiller du Roy, secretaire d'estat") | 347-349 | Pierre Brulart | "Monsieur, vous verrez par ce que porte le Sr de Balagny …" | none |
| 188r-v (address 189v "A Monsieur Monsieur de Lanssac, chevalier des ordres du Roy") | 350-353 | Louis de Saint-Gelais, sieur de Lanssac (the father) | "Monsieur, je n'ay pas grant loisir de faire longue lettre …" | none |

Content in brief (clear): the election of Anjou at the Diet; Balagny (Monluc's son) carries the news; Lanssac asks the
King to excuse a short letter, says he was employed to the end in "ceste negotiation", and to Brulart and his father
asks for favour and recompense for the costs he has borne. The 9 May letter in the cipher is the one to Charles IX,
f. 174 (canvas 329), already read in session 2 (reading_siblings.txt).
