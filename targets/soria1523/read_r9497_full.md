## Running text

Andrea Doria and the Prince of Salerno are discontented with the King [of France], because he had promised them
the enterprise of this city and gave it to the Bastard of Savoy. And one who calls himself Jeronimo Doria, a
kinsman of the leading men of this city, told me on behalf of the said Andrea Doria that, [if] Your Majesty
[would be willing] to take the said Andrea Doria into your service with his [four] galleys, he will serve you
most willingly, and the said [Jeronimo] will do the same on behalf of the Prince of Salerno, and [?] (clear:
so that I write it to Your Caesarean Majesty and to the viceroy of Naples...).

I have pressed Antonioto Adorno to make this republic pay what they owe Your Majesty under the capitulation
[made] in the [time] of [Pope] Leo. He has told me that he has approached Martin Centurion to [write/speak]
to Your Majesty about this, and that [until] he has your [answer] nothing can be [discussed/done].

## Confidence

High for the cipher in block 1 lines 7-10 and the running shape of block 2 and f.579r (Antonioto Adorno,
republica, capitulacion, Martin Centurion, galeras, servira, mismo, nada all spell out). The code-group
meanings marked ? (pes, xob, paf, tin, xag, xa, sud, sab) are guesses from context. 'Ŀ+α' in 'quatro' is not
L = t: both copies write a tall capital L there and the small angular L for the t (30 Sept 2026). 'z7∞' is read as 'hará' only from the sense. The signs after Salerno, 'ꝑ̄ gto',
are unread (R9498 writes 'yꝑ̄. g+o ∃ ⊙.' there: 'Salerno' then a closer). Block 1 collated with R9498.

## Collation with R9498 (30 Sept 2026)

Alphabetical order of the code: each initial consonant covers a stretch of the plaintext alphabet (z: al, alla,
buena, como, con, de; y: dicho, dizen, el, ella, en, es, esta, esto; x: Francia, gana, guerra, hable, haze, la, le,
lo; t: Magestad, mucho, no, papa, parte; s: para, por, qual, que, rey; p: se, servi-, si, su, tiempo, un, V.,
voluntad, yo). xuf therefore stands between F and L: 'xuf yic' = habla-do (I), not 'dicho' (= yeb).

## Block 1, continuous text (30 Sept 2026)

Andrea Doria y el principe de Salerno estan muy descontentos del rey, porque les avia quitado la enpresa de esta
ciudad y la avia dado al bastardo de Saboya; y a me [hablado] uno que se dize Jeronimo Doria, que es uno de los
principales de esta ciudad, de parte del dicho Andrea Doria, y dizen que si V. Mag. [quiere] aceptar en su
servicio al dicho Andrea Doria con sus quatro galeras, que el lo servira de muy buena voluntad, y lo mismo [hará]
el dicho de parte del principe de Salerno.

Andrea Doria and the Prince of Salerno are very discontented with the King, because he had taken from them the
enterprise of this city and had given it to the Bastard of Savoy; and one who calls himself Jeronimo Doria, one of
the leading men of this city, has [spoken] to me on behalf of the said Andrea Doria, and they say that if Your
Majesty [will] accept the said Andrea Doria into your service with his four galleys, he will serve you very
willingly, and the said [Jeronimo] will [do] the same on behalf of the Prince of Salerno.

Grades: all words H/C except [hablado] (xuf yic, I from alphabetical position and sense), [quiere] (sob 7., M) and
[hará] (z7∞, I). 'bastardo' is ɡ̣∞ƀLαc + yic (-do); 'estan' is ymf (esta) + y (n).
